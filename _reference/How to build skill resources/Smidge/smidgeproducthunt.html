Hey Product Hunt! I'm Albert, and I built Smidge because your agents need their "I know Kung-Fu moment" too. The Agent Skills spec is now supported across 30+ AI platforms. There's a growing ecosystem of skills, but the tooling to create them from real knowledge didn't exist. With Smidge, you drop in a YouTube masterclass, a 500-page PDF, your team's internal wiki, or all of them at once (up to 50MB per file). Source-aware extraction reads each format the way a human expert would, and for large inputs, it automatically detects topics and produces a full catalogue of focused skills. A 45MB sales training manual became 5 separate skills: methodology, objection handling, pipeline management, negotiation, and team coaching. A 14-minute CSS tutorial became a 1,847-word production skill in 12 seconds. Would love to know: what expertise would you want your agent to have?
